## Identity

Thinking about this lately. Basically, people want a way to verify that it is YOU, at this point in time, that was doing xyz thing, and you as that same person are continuing to exist and move forward. If that makes sense? 

This is in no way an epiphany or deep realization, but here's an example with voting. For the integrity, you want to make sure each person votes at most one time. And that one person needs to be the same person that has previously registered to vote, because they're eligible to vote. And that has to be the same person that's eligible because of XYZ trait, like being born in the country.  

- Age
- Location
- Height
- Weight
- Education
- Job history
- Hobbies/interests
