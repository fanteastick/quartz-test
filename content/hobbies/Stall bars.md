## Summary of some exercises

[Unleashing the Power of Stall Bars: A Complete Swedish ladder Workout – Trainnox](https://trainnox.com/blogs/all/unleashing-the-power-of-stall-bars-a-complete-stall-bar-workout-tips)

Hanging Knee raises/leg raises

- can be sideways, straight forward, legs bent, legs straight

Wall bar pull-ups: they're basically just normal pull ups

Wall ladder tricep extensions

- may be too easy, and if so, you can add a weighted vest
- ![[Stall bars_image_1.png|300]]

Hanging leg curls: face towards the wall and curl your legs behind you, which will work the hamstrings. You can add a weight between your feet. 

Dead hang: forearm grip muscles? The main goal is to improve muscle endurance. 

"Lateral hip raises supported on the elbow" - like a side plank, but your feet are on the ladder. See pic: 

![[Stall bars_image_2.png|300]]

There's a few more arm things that you can do e.g. rows with resistance bands, but I got tired so I will stop here.
