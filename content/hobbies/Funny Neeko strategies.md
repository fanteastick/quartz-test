## Monsters

Transform into a minion to sneakily gank by walking straight up to them. 

- hitting turret as a minion forces you to transform back to Neeko
- To get closer to the enemy, become the first melee minion. 
- If you become a ranged minion, it's better to be the first out of all of them, because the enemy will start counting them as the minions come into view. 

Transform into scuttle crab to confuse the enemy jungler when they're doing an objective in the pits. 

Transform into the small raptor or wolf so you can walk around the minimap without an indicator. 

Transform into a plant and wait in the jungle for a jumpscare. 

Transform into the red/blue buff for maximum chaos. 

## Ultimate

Simple idea: you will be able to land it on more people (so satisfying!) if you don't give them the opportunity to escape.

1) Root before ultimate
2) Flash + ultimate
3) Flash + rocketbelt + ultimate --> crosses a surprising amount of distance
4) w + ultimate --> so they don't see specifically where you are, although they can probably guess. 
5) transformation into ultimate --> the enemy won't see the initial animation until they get knocked up. 

There's actually a short knock-up on the ultimate, so you can synergize with a Yasuo. 

## Clone misc

Send out your clone and start an animation such as taunt or recall. The clone will persist through the end of the animation, which is longer than a typical clone. 

Stop your character for half a second to mimic a clone. 

Use your clone to block enemy abilities such as Caitlyn ult.
