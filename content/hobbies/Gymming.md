> Health is wealth. 💬 Ancient proverb (?)

I'm a big fan of [Hevy](https://www.hevyapp.com/), which is like strava but for weightlifting. It has a bunch of exercises, video diagrams, and a log and workout tracker.

## Rotations, or things to target

It's good to take a few rest days throughout the week too! 

### Legs

- squat - although it's kind of a full-body workout
- hamstring curl
- leg extension
- calf raises
- deadlift
### Back

- incline rows
- seated row
- lat pulldown

Most back exercises with the barbell will include "hinging at the waist", which means you lean forward, but keep your weight steady, and keep your whole back straight. Remember to also look forward and down. 

### Chest

- bench press
- incline press
### Not really a rotation but Abs

- candlesticks
- twists
- curls / sit-ups
- plank, push ups, side planks
	- with side planks, you can do thread the needles
	- proper push up form: [How To Do Push Ups with Antranik - YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4dF1DOWzf20) [The Ultimate Guide To Push Ups \| Gymshark Central](https://www.gymshark.com/blog/article/how-to-do-a-push-up#how-to-do-push-ups-correctly) 
	- Variations on the push up: incline push ups --> decline push ups --> diamond push ups --> plyo push ups
- mountain climbers
- eyes, why's, and tees
### Cardio options
- run
- swim
- walk
- bike
