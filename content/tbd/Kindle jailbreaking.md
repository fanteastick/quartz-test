## Winterbreak

[Home \| Kindle Modding Wiki](https://kindlemodding.org/)

This is basically the official guide. I restarted my kindle like 20x trying to get this to work. Make sure you disable automatic updates, since it stops working at kindle version 5.18+. 

It wasn't working for me because at the install hotfix section, my kindle was complaining that there's a mismatch in the cc.db which tracks what's in your library. Despite resetting my kindle 2x, it's still there. 

Also when you do `;log mrpi` you'll know it worked if some text that says "Hush little baby" pops up over the spacebar, and then the icons persist. If you only see a box and then that flashes out, and then nothing, then it probably crashed.

## Email your stuff to your kindle

[Learn About Sending Documents to Your Kindle Library - Amazon Customer Service](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=G5WYD9SAF7PGXRNA) 

Find your kindle email: Go to your Amazon account's Manage Your Content and Devices → Devices tab → Select your Kindle → Find the email under “Send-to-Kindle E-mail Settings”.

In Amazon's Personal Document Settings (under Preferences), add the email you will send from (e.g., your Gmail) to the Approved Personal Document E-mail List. Otherwise, Amazon will reject the emailed documents.

Alternatively go to this webpage ([Send to Kindle](https://www.amazon.com/sendtokindle)) while logged in and you can drop it in. Then after a few minutes, on your kindle, it'll be in your library and you can download it for reading.
