Premise: Why is it beneficial or important for individuals to run AI locally on their own devices, when it comes faster and more powerfully if provided over the cloud?

## Scaffold

- The illusion of choice - you can pick your own model, or if you're really good, fine-tune your own model
- Privacy - your data is not stored
- Enhance **everything** you do on your machine
- Maybe unhackable? No censorship for sure
## Cons

- power consumption
- Laptop or home server processing power is weaker than enterprise solutions
	- Thus, slow

## Viable options

- Apple's M4 chips - 38 TOPS NPU
- AI laptops - AMD Ryzen AI 9, Intel Core Ultra
- Discrete GPU within laptops
- Self-hosted GPU farm, a la PewDiePie
