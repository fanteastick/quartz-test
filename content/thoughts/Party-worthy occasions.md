Inspiration list for parties, and genres of party that I've been noticing in my phase of life.

# Life events
- Housewarming <-- probably most common
- Marriage or family related: e.g. engagement party, wedding, baby shower
- Birthday, usually a surprise
# Holidays
- Halloween - ok to either host a costume party, or go clubbing in costume days/weeks before
- Independence day - color theme becomes very big
- Thanksgiving and Christmas --> Friendsgiving, Friendsmas
- Valentine's day --> Palentine's day

# Themes and party types
- Sophisticated dinner party - everyone chip in
- Casual potluck - usually dinner, usually ok to be a bit late
- Costume/dress-up theme, like Halloween, or "everyone dress up as your ideal type"
- Outdoor picnic
- BBQ, + optional pool
- Classic house party
	- Beware of noise complaints
