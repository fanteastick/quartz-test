[Reddit - All Poketch Apps w/ Locations](https://www.reddit.com/r/PokemonBDSP/comments/r6yi38/all_poketch_apps_w_locations/)

> [!web]-
> Digital Clock - Default app when Poketch is obtained
> 
> Calculator - Default app when Poketch is obtained
> 
> Memo Pad - From President of Poketch Company in Jubilife City, after obtaining 1st Gym Badge.
> 
> Pedometer - Default app when Poketch is obtained
> 
> Pokemon Party List - Default app when Poketch is obtained
> 
> Friendship Checker - Talk to Aroma Lady in Eterna City Pokemon Center
> 
> Dowsing Machine - On first time entering upper part of Route 207(from Cycling Road)
> 
> Egg Monitor - After placing a Pokemon in the Nursery, talk to the man sitting by the table inside the Nursery to receive the new Poketch app.
> 
> Pokemon History - From Ruin Maniac southwest of the Pokemon Center in Solaceon Town
> 
> Counter - From cashier directly in front of escalator on 2F of Veilstone Department Store
> 
> Analog Watch - From trainer inside southwestern-most house of Celestic Town
> 
> Marking Map - From President of Poketch Company in Jubilife City, after obtaining 3rd Gym Badge.
> 
> Coin Toss - From Rich Boy in Valor Lakefront suite that can only be accessed by Rock Climb on Route 213
> 
> Calendar - From Poketch developer in house near Pokemon Rock in Sunyshore City after showing him a Pokemon with a Serious nature
> 
> Dot Artist - From Poketch developer in house near Pokemon Rock in Sunyshore City after showing him a Pokemon with a Naive nature
> 
> Spinner - From Poketch developer in house near Pokemon Rock in Sunyshore City after showing him a Pokemon with a Quirky nature
> 
> Chain Counter - From Professor Oak upon first entering Ramanas Park
> 
> Kitchen Timer - From girl at Ramanas Park after showing her a Snorlax
> 
> Color Changer - From the same girl at Ramanas Park after obtaining the above app then showing her a Kecleon
> 
> Hidden Moves - From Hiker at entrance of Oreburgh Gate
>
