## Chores and tracking

- After going grocery shopping, take a photo of the receipt. Then it should extract what I bought, acknowledge that it's in my fridge starting at x time, warn me later down the line when my items are going bad. Also it should triage the spending from any receipts into a budget tracker

## Personal notes

- Take three notes of related but similar topics and reorganize it so the separate topics are clear. E.g. the pages in [[tags/resourceland]] are quite disorganized. 
- Notes on people that I meet, like a networking helper. Find out latest details that they've posted online e.g. LinkedIn, and also remember things we talked about previously so I can follow up another time.
- In general, for personal life stuff, it *might* be easier to have voice input interface or something, so I can just say out loud what I need the AI assistant to do while I'm driving or something similar. 

## Surfing the web

- Online shopping: given my criteria for an item of clothing and a price range, find 5 options for me. And if I don't like it, go back and forth asking for specifics, and continue presenting more options. 
- Also online shopping: passively scan certain things that are on my to-buy list, and check for sales!
