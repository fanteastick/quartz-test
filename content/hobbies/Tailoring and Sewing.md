## Basic tailoring

- Use pins to hold the waist at the sizing that you want. Then you can kind of sew down the sides, which creates these pockets of excess that you can clip out. 
- Making things shorter, cutting off sleeves, changing strap lengths
- Punch in grommets to make a corset-type closing
- Adding elastic
- Add a lining

## Patterning

- Can buy patterns from fabric stores
- Working with patterns is a whole skillset to perfect
	- modification, iteration, size ranges

## Basic fixing

- zippers
- open seams
- hemming
