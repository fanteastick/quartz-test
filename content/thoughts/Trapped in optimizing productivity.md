> Not seeing the forest for the trees, or something. 💬 a quote.

One thing that is easy to fall into for well-meaning but easily distracted productivity yearners is to get stuck in the method of improving productivity, while making no moves to actually get the work done. You can spend all this time and money trying different tools, buying memberships, setting up your systems, but all you really need is something that's usable, and move on. Getting the work done is better than having a nice system for getting the work done.

Examples of productivity-yearning

- [OpenClaw — Personal AI Assistant](https://openclaw.ai/) endlessly customizing your AI agent
- Notion page-building
- Scrolling for obsidian plugins
- Top 10 best study apps for iPad

Examples of forgoing systems in favor of getting work done

- Ignoring if your files are messy or not aesthetic
- Having text-searchable rather than visually organized data
- use the built-in and default systems

Quick ways to increase productivity without wasting too much time

- If there's a repeated action or button you keep using, set up a keyboard shortcut or gesture
- Instead of making sure the system is working for all your possible things, just get it functional for the most immediate task at hand
- Eisenhower matrix everything away

|               | Urgent   | Not Urgent |
| ------------- | -------- | ---------- |
| Important     | Do       | Decide     |
| Not important | Delegate | Delete     |

- Do it now
- Delegate it to someone else
- Decide when you'll do it
- Delete it off your to-do list
