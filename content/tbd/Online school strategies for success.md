## general lifestyle

- each week, figure out what you need to do for that week, and reserve time after work/on weekends
- reduce hobbies, socializing, vacations
- 20-25hr/wk on school
- start projects early

> "You can and should sometimes choose to do more than the minimum requirement, because it's an enrichment opportunity. But you should also learn where the line is and how to do just enough work to get an A. Not because you're lazy, but because investing time in one thing makes that time unavailable for another thing, so you have to choose wisely."

> Don't feel like you have to learn only from the materials provided to you... It's not disloyal to go watch YouTube videos from MIT or Stanford. Your job is to learn the material by hook or by crook.
